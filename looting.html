Posters from the garbage wind collection

Berlin, DE9 min readArtist: Zuzanna Czebatul

The posters from the Trash Wind collection owe their name to the overall style of the images and what they are made of.

This is a completely different perspective on the things we encounter on a daily basis, and I want to show that this is where beauty lies.

There is nothing more beautiful than simple things that evoke a deeper sense than the pompous masks we wear every day....
