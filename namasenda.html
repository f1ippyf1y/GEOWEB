The Commune is a damn cool place

Voronezh. Russia7 min readMusician: Namasenda

A year earlier, Vinzavod was opened near the railway station, on the territory of a vodka factory. Apparently, 40-degree drinks were no longer popular among young people, and the factory went bankrupt.

The interior design was inspired by the Vinzavod factory in Moscow. It was the first creative space of its kind in the city.

The bar is beautifully decorated in a loft-style. But the atmosphere there is more like a noisy drinking establishment, while the Commune is an open-air nightclub with a stage, cocktail bars, and a restaurant.
